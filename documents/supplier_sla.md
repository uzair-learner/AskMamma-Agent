# Supplier Service Levels

Northwind Office Supply normally delivers office goods within five business days.

FreshPack Foods normally delivers refrigerated and shelf-stable food products within three business days. Expiry dates must be checked at receiving.

TechCore Components normally delivers electronics within ten business days. Barcode scanners and peripherals should be tested before stocking.

MedSupply Direct normally delivers medical supplies within four business days. Lot numbers and expiry dates should be recorded.
