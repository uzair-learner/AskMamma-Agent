AskMamma Piadineria Food Safety Records

AskMamma holds a current HACCP food safety certificate for preparation, storage, and service of piadina ingredients. The certificate covers chilled dairy handling, cured meat storage, allergen separation, daily surface sanitation, and temperature logging.

The restaurant team completed local food safety training with the municipal hospitality office. The training covered safe receiving procedures, hand-washing controls, cross-contamination prevention, and documented closing checks.

AskMamma keeps weekly internal audit notes for refrigerator temperature checks, supplier delivery review, and allergen labeling accuracy.
