Inventory Pilot AI Owner History

Inventory Pilot AI was created as a local-first agent application for demonstrating practical AI assistant workflows with tools, memory, document retrieval, tracing, and evaluation.

The name comes from the idea of asking a helpful assistant for grounded answers instead of guessing. The project focuses on clear setup, reproducible local data, and testable agent behavior.

Inventory Pilot AI continues to evolve around modular backend APIs, a simple UI, seed scripts, and reliable tests.
