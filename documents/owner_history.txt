AskMamma Owner History

AskMamma was founded by Marta Bellini, a second-generation restaurateur from Emilia-Romagna. Marta learned piadina preparation from her family bakery before opening a small digital-first piadineria focused on fresh fillings, transparent sourcing, and quick delivery.

The restaurant name comes from regular customers asking Marta for recommendations by saying, "Ask mamma what goes best today." The phrase became the brand name when the restaurant expanded its online ordering service.

Marta continues to lead supplier selection and menu quality checks.
