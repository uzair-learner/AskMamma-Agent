# Return Policy

Customers may return unopened products within 30 days of delivery. Perishable food products can only be returned if the cold-chain seal was broken during delivery or if the product arrived damaged.

Electronics may be returned within 45 days if the serial number matches the original order record. Medical supplies must remain sealed and cannot be returned after expiry.

Operations staff should document every return in the movement log and quarantine returned goods until quality review is complete.
